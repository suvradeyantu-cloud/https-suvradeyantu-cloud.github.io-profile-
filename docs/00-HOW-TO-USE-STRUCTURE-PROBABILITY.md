## 🎯 Who this is for

(BMU/BSMMU, July 2026 / 2027 session) and benchmarking against BSMMU Residency
(Orthopaedics) MCQ. It is a *suggestion + rapid-revision* book, **not** a substitute
for reading the standard textbooks listed at the end.
